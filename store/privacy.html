UniSS privacy policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026. Applies to the UniSS browser extension (Manifest V3) on Chromium browsers and Firefox.

What UniSS does

UniSS captures the visible area or a full web page, then lets you download, copy, or annotate the image. It runs entirely in the browser. There is no UniSS account and no UniSS server.

Data UniSS handles

When you capture a page, UniSS reads the pixels of the active tab (HTTP or HTTPS only) and stores the resulting image locally so you can edit, copy, or download it. Settings such as capture mode, image format, quality, and interface language are also stored locally.

Storage keys live in the browser’s extension storage. Settings and editor handoff use storage.local, including unissMode, unissFormat, unissQuality, unissAutoCaptureOnClick, unissLocale, unissPageInfoBar (page title/URL bar setting), and temporary editor fields unissEditImage and unissEditTs. Settings Clear temporary captures removes only the four temporary Region/Edit keys; it does not remove settings such as unissPageInfoBar. Region capture also uses a short-lived unissRegionStash key (PNG snapshot + viewport metrics in storage.local) that is removed after Copy/Download/Edit, cancel, or page unload, and is refused if older than about five minutes. Related temporary Region keys such as unissRegionTabId may also appear during an in-progress selection. Expired Region stash (~5 minutes) and Edit handoff (~30 minutes) keys are also removed proactively on the service worker’s onInstalled/onStartup and early in popup init, without an alarms permission.

What we do not collect

UniSS does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment details, location, browsing history, or analytics. It does not sell data, use data for advertising, or share data with third parties. Captures are not uploaded.

Network

UniSS does not contact remote servers. The only fetches are packaged language files inside the extension (i18n/messages/*.json) via runtime.getURL. There are no remote scripts, CDNs, or trackers.

Permissions

  • activeTab — temporary access to the tab you invoke UniSS on, to capture that tab.
  • scripting — injects local helper functions for full-page scrolling and restoring the page. No remote code.
  • storage — keeps settings and the editor image on your device.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you clear it (for example by removing the extension or clearing extension storage). Uninstalling UniSS removes its local storage. Downloaded files are ordinary files you save; UniSS does not control those copies.

Children

UniSS is a screenshot tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If UniSS ever starts sending data off-device, this policy will be updated before that change ships, and store privacy disclosures will be updated to match.
